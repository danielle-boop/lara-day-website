Admissions

Join the Lara Day School family

We welcome applications from parents who are seeking a warm, safe, and stimulating learning environment for their children. Our admission process is simple and transparent, ensuring every child has a smooth start to their learning journey.

Step 1

Visit the school or contact us to make enquiries.

Step 2

Complete the admission form and submit required documents.

Step 3

Child assessment and parent interaction (where applicable).

Step 4

Admission offer and payment of fees.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed admission form
  • Copy of child’s birth certificate
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Medical report / immunization record
  • Previous school report (if applicable)
