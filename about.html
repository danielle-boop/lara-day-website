About Lara Day School

Where learning begins with love and care.

Lara Day School is a caring and child-focused educational institution committed to nurturing young minds in a safe, joyful, and stimulating environment. We believe that every child deserves the opportunity to grow academically, socially, and emotionally at their own pace.

Our curriculum combines strong academic foundations with creative activities that encourage curiosity, confidence, and independence. At Lara Day School, children learn through play, exploration, and guided discovery.

Our Mission

To deliver quality early childhood education that builds confident and happy learners.

Our Vision

To be a trusted day school known for excellence, care, and strong values.

Our Values

Love, respect, discipline, creativity, and teamwork.
